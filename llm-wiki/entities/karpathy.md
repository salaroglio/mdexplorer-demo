# Andrej Karpathy

## Summary

Andrej Karpathy is an artificial intelligence researcher, co-founder of OpenAI and former Director of AI at Tesla. In April 2026 he published a gist on GitHub that describes the **LLM Wiki** pattern, an approach to knowledge management where an AI agent actively maintains a structured markdown wiki instead of running retrieval on raw documents at every query.

## Key points

- He was a co-founder of OpenAI [source: [sources/2026-04-karpathy-gist](../sources/2026-04-karpathy-gist.md)]
- He was Director of AI at Tesla, where he led the Autopilot team [source: public, Twitter/X profile]
- In April 2026 he proposed the **LLM Wiki** pattern in a public gist [source: [sources/2026-04-karpathy-gist](../sources/2026-04-karpathy-gist.md)]
- His personal wiki has reached 100 articles and 400,000 words [source: [sources/2026-04-beyond-rag-article](../sources/2026-04-beyond-rag-article.md)]
- Signature phrase (adapted to the foundation you use): *"\[Markdown editor\] is the IDE; the LLM is the programmer; the wiki is the codebase"* — with MdExplorer it becomes: *"MdExplorer is the IDE; the LLM is the programmer; the wiki is the codebase"* [source: [sources/2026-04-karpathy-gist](../sources/2026-04-karpathy-gist.md)]
