# Minutes of the steering committee, 18 September 2026

## TL;DR

The committee approved the start of the pilot with a smaller scope than the one written in the requirements.
It also asked to settle the privacy issue before any operator sees a draft. Four actions remain open, each
with an owner and a date.

- Pilot approved: **6 weeks**, **12 operators**.
- The text of the tickets must be **anonymised** before it leaves the network, or the model must be hosted in the company.
- Next committee on Friday 23 October 2026, with the numbers of the shadow phase.

## Present

Marta Colombo (sponsor), Giulia Ferraris (help desk), Paolo Gentile (DPO), Luca Bianchi (information systems).

## What was discussed

**Scope.** Marta Colombo asked to reduce the load on the help desk in the period when the heating systems are
switched on, the busiest of the year. It was decided to shorten the pilot and to involve fewer people.

**Privacy.** Paolo Gentile pointed out that the text of the tickets contains names, addresses and sometimes
tax codes. Sending it as it is to a model in the cloud does not meet requirement R6.

**Trust of the operators.** Giulia Ferraris proposed to have every draft rated with a single click, so as not
to add work. Everybody liked the proposal.

## Decisions

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | The pilot is approved |
| D2 | It lasts **6 weeks** |
| D3 | **12 operators** of the day shift take part |
| D4 | No ticket text leaves the network without being **anonymised** |
| D5 | Drafts are rated with one click: accepted, edited, discarded |
