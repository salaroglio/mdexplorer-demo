# Nordica Crediti procedures

## TL;DR

This page **simulates** the supplier area of the Nordica Crediti website, where the contracting authority publishes the procedures a
company can take part in. In the demo it is a file of the project; in a real company it would be the real website, and the account
manager's agent would read it with a browsing tool.

- Five procedures: two already seen by Pentagroup and three new ones.
- Each procedure has a code, a deadline and a tender document.
- Updated on **8 March 2027**.
